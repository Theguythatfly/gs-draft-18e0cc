Explore ourcfDNA solutions

A small blood sample can reveal a world of information

Cell-free DNA, or cfDNA, consists of tiny fragments of DNA released into the bloodstream as cells naturally renew. These fragments carry biological signals from different parts of the body, offering a unique window into what is happening inside us.

By analyzing the amount, sequence, patterns, and physical characteristics of cfDNA, healthcare professionals can uncover important health insights, sometimes before changes become visible through symptoms or medical imaging.

At Gene Solutions, we transform these tiny signals into meaningful insights across prenatal healthcare, cancer screening, and precision oncology, helping people and their doctors make informed decisions sooner.

Discover Our AI& Data Engine

  • Advancing the frontiers of cell-free DNA

    DNAsphere.AI is the data and artificial intelligence engine powering Gene Solutions’ precision cell-free DNA platform, transforming vast multi-omic datasets into biological insights and scalable applications for clinicians and patients worldwide.

  • Multimodal Intelligence

    Advanced machine-learning and deep-learning models analyze multiple dimensions of cell-free DNA, including genomic, epigenomic and fragmentomic features, to identify patterns that may not be visible through individual signals alone.

  • Data at Scale

    More than 300,000 multi-omic cell-free DNA profiles provide a growing foundation for research and innovation across early cancer detection, precision oncology and reproductive health.

  • From Discovery to Application

    DNAsphere.AI helps translate data into new biological insights and scalable applications, from investigating early cancer-associated fragmentomic changes to identifying pregnancy-related risks patterns, enabling efficient, transferable and clinically relevant cfDNA solutions.
